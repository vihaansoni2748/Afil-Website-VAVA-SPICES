Our Story

Four decades rooted in Kerala's spice hills

Founded in the early 1980s by K.P. Mathai & Sons under the name Kollarmalil Spices, our family has spent generations trading the finest produce of the Western Ghats.

In 2015 the legacy was reborn as Vava Spices (P) Ltd — combining old-world sourcing relationships with modern grading, packaging and quality control.

From the cardamom estates of Idukki to the tea gardens of the high ranges, we bring authentic, aromatic spices to kitchens and businesses across the country.

  1. 1980s

    Kollarmalil Spices

    K.P. Mathai & Sons trade out of the Kerala hills, building relationships with estate families that still supply us today.

  2. 2015

    Vava Spices (P) Ltd

    The legacy is rebuilt as a private limited company — same sourcing, new grading hall, lab checks and export-grade packing.

  3. Today

    Hills to kitchens

    Household packs and bulk contracts across India, every lot traceable back to the estate it came from.

    How we work

    From the estate gate to your order

    Four stages, every lot, no exceptions. This is the part the video never shows you.

    1. 01

      Source

      Bought direct from estate families at the gate price during the season. We pay above the local rate and we pay on time.

    2. 02

      Grade

      Screen size, colour, moisture and defect rate checked on a sample from every batch. What falls out of grade goes to the cheaper tier, never into your pack.

    3. 03

      Pack

      Whole spice, packed whole, in food-grade liners with the lot code and packing date printed on every unit.

    4. 04

      Dispatch

      Lot numbers travel with the shipment, so a complaint can be traced to a bag and answered with a fact rather than an apology.

    Standards

    What we will put in writing

    Documentation is available on request with your first order — batch sheets, moisture readings, test reports and the lot trail back to the estate.

    • Food-grade packaging

      Barrier liners that let whole spice breathe without letting moisture in.

    • Lot traceability

      Every pack stamped with a lot code tied to its grading record.

    • Nothing ground

      We ship whole pods, berries, buds and bark. Grinding is your choice, not ours.

    • Moisture & cleanliness checks

      Sampled and recorded before a batch is released for despatch.
